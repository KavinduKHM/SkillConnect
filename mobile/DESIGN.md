## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an encouraging, tactile, and radiant atmosphere tailored for bite-sized skill acquisition and community knowledge sharing. The design direction merges **Warm Tactile Minimalism** with **Organic Softness**: clean structural compositions grounded in warm earthen undertones, approachable pill-shaped metrics, and tactile feedback states that make learning feel manageable and celebratory rather than academic or demanding.

### Audience & Mood
- **Target Audience:** Curious adult learners, casual upskillers, and community mentors engaging during micro-moments throughout their day (commutes, morning coffee, wind-down periods).
- **Emotional Signature:** Welcoming, radiant, mentally unburdened, and rewarding. Interfaces must feel baked in natural sunlight—never sterile, clinical, or overly tech-forward.
- **Design Philosophy:** Frictionless bite-sized modules, high legibility under varying ambient lighting, and tangible satisfaction upon sprint completions through soft warmth and dimensional tactility.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from terracotta clay, ripe apricots, golden raw honey, and sun-warmed sandstones, anchored by a deep roasted-espresso neutral for effortless contrast.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#D95D39` - Burnt Terracotta):** The dominant brand and focal action driver. Applied to primary sprint progression trackers, focal interactive CTAs, and active navigation nodes.
- **Secondary (`#E07A5F` - Sunlit Coral):** Supportive interactive accent used for secondary actions, interactive highlight rings, and peer engagement cues (e.g., claps, mentor comments).
- **Tertiary (`#E5A93C` - Honey Amber):** Celebration, streak counters, mastery badges, time estimations, and micro-sprint completion glows.
- **Neutral Core (`#2B211E` - Espresso Hearth):** High-legibility text base ensuring AA/AAA contrast ratios against light backgrounds without the stark harshness of pure black.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - `surface-canvas`: `#FAF6F0` (Soft Warm Sand)
  - `surface-card`: `#FFFDF9` (Soft Ivory)
  - `surface-muted`: `#F3ECE2` (Warm Oat Cream)
  - `border-subtle`: `#EADBCE` (Terracotta-tinted Sand Line)

## Typography

The type system relies exclusively on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric clarity, welcoming rounded apertures, and wide counters to maintain legibility during fast micro-reads on handheld displays.

### Usage Hierarchy
- **Display & Headlines:** Set tight with subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (Bold to ExtraBold) to assert a punchy, optimistic presence without screaming.
- **Body Content:** Kept at standard regular to medium weights with generous line heights (`1.45` to `1.55`) to facilitate effortless scan-reading of compact instructional steps.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** High-contrast weights (600/700) with slight positive tracking applied to category pills, sprint timers, and skill-level indicator tags.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 8-point layout rhythm guides all spatial distribution, calibrated primarily for thumb-zone efficiency on mobile screens, shifting to fluid multi-column cards on larger viewports.

### Grid & Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `16px` outer margins and `16px` gutters. Floating action buttons and primary sprint triggers stay tethered within the bottom 35% safe zone.
- **Tablet (640px–1024px):** 8-column layout with `32px` margins and `24px` gutters. Lesson sidebars and peer feedback panels unlock side-by-side configurations.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column constrained fluid canvas maxing out at `1120px` width, anchored by `48px` page margins.

### Spatial Rhythms
- Use `space-xs` (8px) for tightly coupled avatar-and-name pairings and chip badge internals.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding, input fields, and standard list row separations.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) for distinct content groupings within a single learning module.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids cold, detached drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **Warm Ambient Halos** combined with layered **Tonal Stepping** and crisp organic borders.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF6F0`):** Background layer providing a soft, paper-like warmth.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#FFFDF9`):** Interactive cards and sprint modules resting over the canvas, surrounded by a 1px border of `#EADBCE`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating / Active Sheets):** Modals, bottom sheets, and elevated sticky controls (`#FFFFFF`).

### Ambient Shadow Characteristics
- **Level 1 (Resting Card Elevation):** `0px 3px 12px -2px rgba(105, 62, 42, 0.05), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(105, 62, 42, 0.03)`. A diffuse terracotta-tinted drop that anchors elements gently.
- **Level 2 (Active / Lifted / Popover):** `0px 10px 24px -4px rgba(105, 62, 42, 0.10), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(105, 62, 42, 0.04)`. Evokes tactile lift when selecting a quiz option or dragging a practice card.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Bottom Trays):** `0px -6px 20px 0px rgba(105, 62, 42, 0.06)`. Smooth upward illumination keeping actions anchored above background scrolls.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, natural rounding that strikes a blend between tactile friendliness and clean structure.

### Radius Schema
- **Base Components (`rounded-md` - 8px / 0.5rem):** Checkboxes, form inputs, tooltips, and compact inline elements.
- **Medium Structural Cards (`rounded-lg` - 16px / 1rem):** Primary skill-sprint cards, module tiles, and comment sheets.
- **Large Elevated Containers (`rounded-xl` - 24px / 1.5rem):** Hero lesson overview banners, full-width modal dialogs, and persistent bottom sheets.
- **Micro-Badges & Interactive Targets (`rounded-full` - 9999px):** All chip triggers, sprint timer indicators, category selectors, and primary buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background in Burnt Terracotta (`#D95D39`), high-contrast text in Ivory (`#FFFDF9`), full pill shape (`rounded-full`), height `52px` for mobile thumb accessibility. Pressed state compresses down by 2% (`scale(0.98)`) and shifts background to `#BE4D2B`.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface in `#F3ECE2`, border in `1.5px solid #EADBCE`, neutral text (`#2B211E`).
- **Tertiary Ghost Button:** Transparent background, text in Terracotta (`#D95D39`), underline on hover, subtle honey tint background on press (`#FEF8EC`).

### Microlearning Sprint Chips
- **Time/Status Chip:** Pill shape (`rounded-full`), padding `6px 14px`. Pale honey background (`#FDF3DC`) with rich amber text (`#9C6A08`) for lesson duration (e.g., "⚡ 3 min read").
- **Interactive Tag Chip:** Neutral Sand background (`#F3ECE2`) with Espresso text (`#2B211E`). When selected, transitions to Terracotta (`#D95D39`) with Ivory text.

### Cards (Learning Modules & Feed)
- Container styled in soft Ivory (`#FFFDF9`), framed with a crisp `1px solid #EADBCE` border, and curved at `16px` (`rounded-lg`).
- Interior layout separates header tag, module title, author pill, and a warm progress line (`#E5A93C` filled, `#EADBCE` track) along the card footer.

### Selection Controls (Radio & Checkboxes)
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular, `22px` diameter, `2px` warm border (`#EADBCE`). Selected state reveals a centered `12px` solid dot in Burnt Terracotta (`#D95D39`).
- **Checkboxes:** Smooth `6px` corner radius, `22px` box. Checked state features a Burnt Terracotta fill with a crisp Ivory checkmark glyph.

### Text Input Fields
- Background in Warm Oat Cream (`#F3ECE2`), transitioning to pure white on focus with a `2px` Terracotta glow (`rgba(217, 93, 57, 0.25)`).
- Generous internal padding (`14px 18px`), paired with floating or top-aligned labels in `#614E45`.

### Skill Progress Tracker
- Segmented linear sprint bars composed of pill-shaped dashes. Completed sprints render in Terracotta, current active sprint pulses in Honey Amber, and locked steps stay muted Sand (`#EADBCE`).
